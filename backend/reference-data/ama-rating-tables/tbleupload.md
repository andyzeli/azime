### Mental Status, Cognition, Highest Integrative Function, and Emotion (MSCHIF-E)

Ref:

Neuro Cog Disorder Scores

None - class 0
• 91–100: Superior functioning in a wide range of activities; life's problems never seem to get out of hand; no symptms.

Class 1
• 81–90: Absent or minimal symptoms; good functioning in all areas; interested and involved in a wide range of activities.
• 71–80: If symptoms are present, they are transient and expectable reactions to psychosocial stressors; slight impairment in social, occupational, or school functioning.

Mild Class 2
• 61–70: Some mild symptoms (e.g., depressed mood and mild insomnia) or some difficulty in social, occupational, or school functioning, but generally functioning pretty well.

Moderate Class 3
• 51–60: Moderate symptoms (e.g., flat affect and circumstantial speech, occasional panic attacks) or moderate difficulty in social, occupational, or school functioning.

Severe Class 4
• Serious symptoms (e.g., suicidal ideation, severe obsessional rituals, frequent shoplifting) or any serious impairment in social, occupational, or school functioning.
• Impairment in reality testing or communication (e.g., speech is at times illogical, obscure, or irrelevant) or major impairment in several areas, such as work or school, family relations, judgment, thinking, or mood.
• Behavior is considerably influenced by delusions or hallucinations or serious impairment in communication or judgment (e.g., sometimes incoherent, acts grossly inappropriately, suicidal preoccupation) or inability to function in almost all areas.
